1. The parser reads dates month first

The shared parser reads every date month first. Tenants that write the day first get their dates flipped: 3 October becomes 10 March. The fix queued for #4471 covers it.

2. The booking-v3 form hints MM/DD/YYYY

Three day-first tenants run booking-v3: alder.physio, copper.studio and pale.harbour. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, so their people type month first. The parser fix does not touch this.

3. Bookings land one day off

Five reports are a move of one day, not a flipped day and month. This points to time zones. The desk's notes list them as one-day moves, but the desk's table files them under the parser by tenant.
